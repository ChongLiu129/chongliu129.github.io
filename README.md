# Chong Liu academic website

Personal academic portfolio, hosted on GitHub Pages at https://chongliu129.github.io/.

## Pages

- `index.html`: profile, projects, publications, design work and academic background.
- `spatial-healing.html`, `children-landscapes.html`, `railway-landscapes.html`: research project details.
- `cv.html`: printable academic CV. Use its Print / Save as PDF button.
- `publications.bib`: citation export for the three featured publications.

## Development

This is a buildless static website. Serve the repository root with a static HTTP server. All assets are local and no third-party scripts, analytics, fonts or build dependencies are required. Publish by updating the existing GitHub Pages source branch.

## Content and image provenance

Professional facts are based on the English CV supplied by Chong Liu in October 2026. Research statuses are preserved: current projects are not presented as completed findings.

- `paper-1.webp`, `paper-2.webp`, `paper-3.webp`: first-page previews rendered from the supplied published PDFs.
- `ufug-cover.webp`: journal cover image embedded in the supplied Urban Forestry & Urban Greening article PDF.
- `hcii-book.webp`: actual cover of HCII 2024, LNCS 14685, rendered from the supplied proceedings PDF.
- The IEEE Access journal panel is a typographic identification panel, not an official issue cover.
- `landscape-analysis.webp` and `green-space.webp`: published figure and landscape-only excerpt from Chong Liu's IEEE Access paper. Used as related methods imagery for current spatial healing work; not presented as a new project result.
- `children-landscapes.webp`: study site figure from Chong Liu's HCII 2024 paper. No identifiable child-participant photographs are used.
- `bicycle-camp.webp`, `river-renewal.webp`, `courtyard.webp`: selected design portfolio sheets supplied by Chong Liu.
- `railway-methods.svg`: original explanatory schematic of the ongoing project's research approach, based on the CV and supplied proposal.

Publication titles and previews link directly to the three publisher URLs supplied by Chong Liu. Images remain subject to their respective author and publisher rights. The supplied CV, project applications, identity material, referee private contact details and signatures are not distributed in this repository.

The visual structure is inspired by the user's reference, SmartScape Design Lab: a wide landscape banner, spacious image-and-text project rows, and publication text alongside journal/book and article previews. Its branding, imagery and research claims are not reused.
