Landscape Perception in Motion

Plateau Landscape Perception and Station–City Cultural Tourism Integration Based on Dynamic Viewing from High-Speed Trains

ROLE

Research Participant

INSTITUTION / SUPPORT

Open Research Fund of the Key Laboratory of Station–City Integration, Tongji University

Research focus

The project connects dynamic visual experiments, plateau landscape resource assessment and station–city tourism applications. My contribution centres on dynamic viewing experiments, eye tracking and statistical modelling.

Approach and contribution

  • Dynamic viewing experiments using railway landscape scenes
  • Eye tracking and statistical modelling of visual perception
  • Linking perceptual evidence with landscape resource assessment

Outcomes and status

Ongoing, 2026–2027. The planned work will inform landscape assessment and the connection between railway scenery, stations and tourism resources.
