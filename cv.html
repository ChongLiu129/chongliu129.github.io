Research Profile

My research examines how the spatial composition and visual qualities of urban and natural environments influence perception, emotional wellbeing and cognitive performance. Integrating quantitative landscape analysis, immersive experiments and multimodal human-factor measurements, I investigate how environmental features shape visual attention, stress recovery and sustained attention, and translate these relationships into evidence-based design guidance.
