Research

Research Interests

Multimodal Human-Factor Measurement

Eye tracking, physiological signals, emotion recognition, and behavioral response analysis for built-environment evaluation.

Spatial Healing Efficacy

Evidence-based identification and optimization of restorative spatial qualities in urban environments.

Environmental Perception and Emotion

Understanding how landscape type, visual quality, and immersive settings influence affective and stress-recovery responses.

Health-Oriented Urban Design

Translating perception and physiological evidence into design guidance for child-friendly and health-supportive landscapes.

Virtual Reality Evaluation

Using immersive environments to test visual, emotional, and recovery responses under controlled experimental conditions.

Interpretable Machine Learning

Applying explainable models to connect visual indicators, human responses, and landscape design decisions.
